Ask COS as setup help

The same engine in two places. Every state uses real Doctor checks, helper actions and answers from the COS Control guide. Pick A or B.

  • Local first. Suggestions come from what COS Control already knows, with no model and no network, so they work with the server down.
  • Fixed actions. Each button is one of COS Control's own commands or settings. Typed words pick an answer; they never become a command.
  • Changes confirm in place. A change shows what it will do first. Restarts wait while a job or recording runs, as they do today.
  • Return keeps today's meaning. With nothing highlighted, Return asks COS and Option-Return files to Work, as it does now.

A. A list that drops from the Ask box

Opens when the box is focused or on ⌘K, and closes on Esc or a click outside. The Today page does not move. Best for quick, one-tap fixes; long answers and progress share the box's width.

B. A setup panel under Today's header

Opens in the page, under the header, and pushes Today down. The list sits on the left and the highlighted item's detail on the right: its answer, its confirm, its progress. It stays open until you close it, so a long repair stays in view.
